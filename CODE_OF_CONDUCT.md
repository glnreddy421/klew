# Code of Conduct

Participation in this project — issues, discussions, pull requests, and other spaces — should stay respectful and professional.

**Expected**

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Accept feedback and own mistakes.

**Not welcome**

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualized language or advances.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Anything else that would be out of place in a professional setting.

Contributions that break this may be edited or removed, and accounts may be blocked. Report problems to glnreddy6@gmail.com. If the report is about the person receiving that address, use [GitHub's abuse reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) instead.

This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
